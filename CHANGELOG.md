# Changelog

Newest first. Every update gets a new version number, an entry here, and its own release notes file
(`.github/changes/<number>.md`).

## v1

### NEW
- The **Voidblade**: a non-combat dimensional utility tool in obsidian and neon violet.
- **Rift Anchor:** sneak + right-click saves your position, right-click returns you. Five slots (`/voidblade slot <1-5>`, alias `/vb`, plus `list` and `clear`), works across dimensions, saved per world. Safe teleportation finds solid, non-hazardous ground and refuses lava, fire and blocked spots.
- **Void Magnet:** hold the Voidblade to pull dropped items and XP orbs within 8 blocks with smooth movement. Living entities are never touched. Crouch to pause it.
- **Portal visuals:** animated decorative purple rifts at both ends of a return, magnet energy trails and a pulsing range ring. Purely visual, nothing is placed in the world.
- Cooldown sweep on the item and a recharge bar in the action bar, custom particles and six custom sounds.
- Crafting recipe: Obsidian, Amethyst Shards, Echo Shard and Ender Pearl.
- Custom pixel-art item texture with a cooldown variant.
